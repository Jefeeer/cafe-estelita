i. — our story

Behind the gray wall, a garden was waiting.

From the street, you could walk right past us — a plain gray facade on busy Summerwind Drive, with only a small hanging logo to give us away. That's exactly how we like it.

Step through the door and the traffic fades into birdsong and soft music. Leaves spill over the walls, light filters through the greens, and there's always a quiet corner with your name on it — indoors where it's cool, or out in the garden where the afternoon slows right down.

Estelita means “little star.” We wanted a place that glows softly rather than shouts: good coffee, honest comfort food, and staff who greet you like family.
